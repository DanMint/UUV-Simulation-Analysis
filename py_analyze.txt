========================================================
       COST-BENEFIT DEFINITIONS (Lance's formula)
========================================================
blue_cost = interceptor ENGAGEMENT spend
          = sum(shots fired * cost per shot)  [hit or miss]
  -> captures the '$2M interceptor vs $1000 drone' trade:
     a HUGIN interceptor's shots are far more expensive than a
     BlueROV2's.

red_cost  = total unit cost of ALL attackers DEPLOYED
          = sum(unitCostMin of every attacker committed)
  -> cost is sunk once a unit is committed, whether it succeeds
     or fails.

loss_exchange_ratio = red_cost / blue_cost
  < 1.0  defence efficient (spent less than it destroyed)
  = 1.0  break-even
  > 1.0  attackers trade up (their loss exceeded your spend)
